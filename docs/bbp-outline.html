Network Analyst Development Framework

Audience: network analysts in cyber defense, and instructors adapting the course

TITLE
Teach the Reasoning, Not Just the Tools
Training that builds analyst judgment through network subject matter.
ACT 1: SETUP
Setting Role
Cyber defense teams need analysts who reason well, not just click through tools. You're an analyst, or an instructor, who wants judgment that holds up under pressure.
Point A (Where am I?) Call to Action (How do I get from A to B?) Point B (Where do I want to be?)
Tool training skips the thinking: framing the question, weighing evidence, and handing off a judgment. Take the self-check, work lessons 01 through 09, and practice with captures. Judgment that others can use and act on.
TRANSITION 1
The course teaches the reasoning through real network work. Three ideas explain it: the approach, the hands-on practice, and the path from facts to judgment.
